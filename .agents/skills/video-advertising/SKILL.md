# Video Advertising Skill

Create premium, professional video advertisements for Element Digital.

## Brand
- Company: Element Digital
- Website: elementdigital.org
- Colors: Navy and white
- Style: Modern, cinematic, professional

## Video Requirements
- Strong attention-grabbing hook
- Realistic, high-quality visuals
- Smooth cinematic transitions
- Natural female voiceover
- Professional background music
- Clear call to action
- 9:16 vertical format
- 15–30 seconds

## Avoid
- Generic AI visuals
- Distorted logos or text
- Choppy editing
- Robotic voices
- Cluttered graphics

## Goal
Create engaging advertisements promoting Element Digital's AI receptionist, automated booking, and CRM services.
